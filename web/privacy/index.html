Your interests

If you answer the optional questions about your interests, we use your answers to recommend places, stories and routes in Passim. If you also agree to personalised offers, we use them to choose which offers and partner deals you see in the app. We don't share your answers with businesses or advertisers. The legal basis is your consent. You can change or delete your answers, and withdraw your consent, at any time in your profile. If you have an account, your answers are stored with it until you delete them or your account; otherwise they are only stored on your phone.
