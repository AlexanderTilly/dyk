Before you ask

Is this just an encyclopedia read aloud?

No. Every story is written to be heard standing there, and read by a real narrating voice rather than your phone's. They are short — the length of the walk to the next corner.

Do I need a connection?

Yes, for now. The stories stream as you reach them, so you'll want data or a local SIM. Downloading a city to carry offline is on its way.

Will it drain my battery?

It follows your position while you're exploring, which costs something — but you can switch exploring off between walks and the app stops looking entirely.

What does it cost?

Nothing today. Every place in Palma is open, with no account and no card.

Which cities?

Palma de Mallorca is finished and narrated. More are being written — but we'd rather have one city done properly than ten cities of silence.
