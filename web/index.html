Before you ask

Is this just an encyclopedia read aloud?

No. Every story is written to be heard standing there, and read by a real narrating voice rather than your phone's.

Do I need a connection?

Yes, for now. Stories stream as you reach them, so you'll want data or a local SIM. Carrying a city offline is on its way.

What does it cost?

Nothing today. Every place in Palma is open, with no account and no card.

When is my city coming?

When it is finished — written, recorded and translated. A city of silent pins helps nobody.
